# One method for every page, each app's own look

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Who decided:** mine (free plugin, the name, the default look, a fixed method with the app's look, light per-project styling); agent's (a craft skill, the role split, derived tokens, the Visuals block as a pointer)

## Context

Pages Solid Vibes shows (explainers, decision pages, plan pages, mockups, tables) followed only a short rule in the generated `## Visuals` block of a project's `AGENTS.md`, and they varied. An earlier attempt in the Pro plugin imposed one house style on every app, so it was dropped.

## Decision

A sixth craft skill, `eli-solid`, in the free plugin. The method is fixed: picture first, real names in mono with a plain caption, one idea per section, two section orders (Explain, Decide), gaps flagged, a go-deeper footer. The look comes from the project, first match wins:

1. a `## Look` section in the project's `AGENTS.md` (outside generated blocks) or `CLAUDE.md`, or the user's words;
2. `docs/design/system.md`;
3. `docs/design/direction.md`;
4. the app's theme tokens;
5. the default: the Solid Vibes look, Notebook in fern green.

Agent's calls:
- It is a craft skill, routed, never called by name. Every skill's `## Visuals` line names it.
- Role split: links and "done" are the primary green; `--hot` ("not done, on the critical path", or the recommended pick) is a derived warm ochre. Red stays for errors. An app's `--hot` that equals its green is re-derived warm.
- The roles fern lacks are derived and marked in the template: `--hot*`, `--warn-tint`, `--line-strong` (3:1), `--cell`, `--off`, three tints, the band.
- The template's visuals block is now a pointer, so there is one method list, in the skill.
- An app with no dark values gets a light-only page with a note.

## Why

One structure makes pages quick to read; the app's own look keeps them from all looking the same. A look kept in a generated block would be overwritten by `init`, so a project's `## Look` lives outside it.

## Alternatives

A Pro-only skill (the owner wants it free). Keeping the bullet rules in the visuals block beside the skill (two lists drift). `--hot` as error red (reads as an error) or as the primary green (clashes with success).

## What it means

Eighteen skills. Every page goes through `eli-solid`. See `docs/how-it-works/craft-skills.md` and `docs/how-it-works/agents.md`.
